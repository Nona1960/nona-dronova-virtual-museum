Nona Dronova — Virtual Art Museum

Painting, the garden, textiles, silver and art education are different forms of a single creative inquiry. The Nona Dronova Virtual Museum brings together the artist's collections, works and materials that reveal the interaction of nature, colour, form and artistic thinking.

About the museum

This museum is being created as an open digital space for encountering the work of Nona Dronova, preserving her works and studying the artistic connections between painting, decorative and applied art and teaching. Each collection keeps its own character; together they make it possible to follow the development of an authorial language and the variety of artistic decisions.

The heart of the museum

The Garden as Art

The garden is a living space of artistic observation. Its colour, light, seasonal changes and compositional rhythms become a source of new images. The Garden as Art section explores the garden as an aesthetic environment in its own right and as a possible laboratory of artistic thinking.

Theory

Botanical Expressionism

At the centre of the museum's research programme is botanical expressionism, the artist's concept of an emotionally expressive depiction of the plant world. The plant is regarded here not only as a visible form but as a source of rhythm, colour relationships, plastic movement and individual experience. The concept is explored through the study of specific works and the comparison of series.

Material and Space

An artistic idea can pass from the painted surface into the texture of fabric, into interior space and into the plastic form of silver. Artistic textiles and the LIBERI jewellery collection are presented as independent directions, each with its own material, technique and artistic task.

Education

The museum regards art education as a space of observation, experiment and the development of an individual visual language. The teaching laboratory will bring together verified materials from teaching practice, methodological work and new educational projects.
