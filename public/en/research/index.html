Research and Publications

The museum's research section is being created for the publication of art-historical observations, the artist's essays, research hypotheses and methodological materials. Particular attention is given to botanical expressionism, the artistic understanding of the garden, interdisciplinary connections and the development of the artist's language. Each publication should carry the author's name, date, sources, the status of the text and a recommended form of citation.

Four levels of text

  • Verified fact — Information confirmed by the artist or a document.
  • Artist's text — Nona Dronova's own words; translations are marked.
  • Museum text — Art-historical interpretation written by the museum.
  • Working hypothesis — An assumption for further testing.

Open data

The museum's catalogue is available in machine-readable form: catalog.json. Record fields draw on Dublin Core elements (title, creator, date, format, source, rights); full CIDOC CRM compliance is not claimed.
