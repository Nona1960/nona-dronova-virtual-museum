LIBERI — The Author's Silver Jewellery Collection

LIBERI is Nona Dronova's authorial collection of silver jewellery. The museum presents it as an independent direction of artistic work with material, scale, plasticity and form. The catalogue will reveal the individuality of each piece through high-quality photographs, information on materials, dimensions and techniques, and the artist's comments. The history of the collection, the origin of its name and its artistic philosophy will be published once agreed with the artist.

LIBERI Rings

A series of handcrafted silver rings, each a single edition: sculptural form, metal and natural stone merge into one composition.

Hearts

Handcrafted silver pendants that turn one of humanity's most enduring symbols into contemporary wearable art. Each pendant is a unique object with its own meaning and emotional expression. Design and making by Nona Dronova.
