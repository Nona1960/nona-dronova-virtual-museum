Theory of Contemporary Painting

The Theory of Contemporary Painting

This section brings together Nona Dronova's theoretical texts on painting — on colour, contrast, brushwork, impasto surface and the expressiveness of plant form. The texts were written by the artist in the course of her work and published in her digital archives; here they are gathered into a single course and set beside the works. For each text the museum adds questions for independent looking.
