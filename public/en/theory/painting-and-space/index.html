IV. Garden, Painting and Space

Painting and Space

Diptych, triptych and a walk through a garden

Diptychs, triptychs and interior panels as a way of turning a room into a single pictorial space through which the eye moves as through a garden.

Nona Dronova · Essay

Original source: the artist's digital archive Large-Interior-Panels-by-Artist-Nona-Dronova, textile-art-and-tapestries

Unlike a single large painting, several interconnected works create a movement of the eye.

The gaze passes from one canvas to another, returns, continues its journey, and gradually begins to perceive the entire room as a unified pictorial space. Colour echoes, recurring botanical forms and the rhythm of individual canvases connect different parts of the interior. The space seems larger than its physical boundaries.

This is how the panels look in the interiors of the artist's own home — genuine photographs.

The interior art panel

The same idea underlies the textile practice: the interior art panel is conceived as an autonomous artistic presence within architectural space rather than as a decorative textile. Such works are capable of defining the atmosphere, rhythm and visual identity of a space.

When the eye wanders through the room like a garden, space becomes infinite.
