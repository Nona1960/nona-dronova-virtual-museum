Glossary

Nona Dronova's own concepts and general painting terms used in the museum's texts.

Dominant contrast
The principal colour opposition around which a composition is organised; other contrasts are subordinate to it (from The Theory of Pictorial Contrasts).
Hierarchy of colour
The principle that colours in a painting are not equally strong: some lead, others support and connect.
Chromatic continuity
The repetition of a colour beyond ‘its’ object, through which colour ‘travels’ across the whole canvas.
Local colour
The object's own colour, without regard to lighting and reflections. A general painting term.
Reflected colour
Colour reflected onto an object from neighbouring surfaces and light. A general painting term.
Impasto
A thick, raised application of paint in which the stroke remains visible. A general painting term.
Impasto Bloom
Nona Dronova's own name for her method and philosophy of layered impasto painting: paint as ‘living matter’.
Painterly matter
The physical fabric of the paint layer — texture, layers, stroke directions — as a carrier of meaning.
Post-Symbolism
The term the artist uses for her own approach to transforming natural form; a working authorial concept.
Botanical expressionism
A proposed authorial term: plant forms as the basis of an expressive statement in colour, rhythm and composition. Not an established art movement.
Interior art panel
A work conceived as an autonomous artistic presence within architectural space.
