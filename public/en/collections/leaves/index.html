Collection

Leaves, Ferns and Hostas

The leaf as an independent subject of art.

Ferns, hostas, rodgersias and irises become studies in form, rhythm and colour. The principle, in the artist's words: “the leaf must remain a leaf — but it must never become boring.”

The collection brings together the Leaves in Painting series and the Leaves, Ferns and Hostas rooms of The Garden as Art catalogue.
