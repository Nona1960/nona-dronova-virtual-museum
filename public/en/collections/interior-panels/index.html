Collection

Large Interior Panels

Diptychs, triptychs and a walk through a garden within one room.

Large-scale works created for interior space. “The gaze passes from one canvas to another, returns, continues its journey, and gradually begins to perceive the entire room as a unified pictorial space. The eye seems to wander through a garden.”

Multi-panel works are shown vertically, one panel above another.
