Collection

Early Works — A Painting Collection

The formation of an artistic language: watercolours, plein-air studies, the first flowers.

The early works make it possible to study how an artistic language took shape: an interest in colour, composition, form and material. The section is built from surviving works and archival records. The chronology and the particulars of the artist's development will be described once the dates have been verified and the works themselves studied.

Early works are not simply a past left behind. They are the roots of Nona Dronova's contemporary art.Nona Dronova
