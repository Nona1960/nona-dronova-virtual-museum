Collection

Lilies and Daylilies

The expression of a single flower: energy, mood, state.

“In this series I approach the lily and daylily not as botanical subjects that must be rendered accurately, but as carriers of energy, mood and emotional states.” A single flower can appear tender and almost vulnerable, then disturbing, mysterious or solemn — and this inner contradiction becomes the central subject of the painting.

The collection also includes the Lilies and Daylilies rooms of The Garden as Art catalogue.
