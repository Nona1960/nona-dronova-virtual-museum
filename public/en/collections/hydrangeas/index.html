Hydrangeas — A Painting Collection

Complex flower heads, colour transitions and repeating forms.

The hydrangea offers the painter a particular world of complex flower heads, colour transitions and repeating forms. The Hydrangeas collection draws the viewer's attention to Nona Dronova's individual decisions: how the composition is organised, how the shades interact, and how a natural motif becomes an independent pictorial image. Specific conclusions are accompanied by images and descriptions of the works.

Unlike the rose, organised around a single energetic centre, the hydrangea is hundreds of individual blossoms forming one unified structure without losing their individuality.Nona Dronova
