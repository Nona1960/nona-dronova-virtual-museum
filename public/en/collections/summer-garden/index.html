The Summer Garden

Irises, poppies, phlox, delphiniums, July and August.

The rooms of The Garden as Art catalogue devoted to the summer garden: Irises, Poppies, July Garden, August, Phlox and Many Flowers. The garden appears here as a succession of flowerings, each bringing its own light, rhythm and colour temperature into the composition.
