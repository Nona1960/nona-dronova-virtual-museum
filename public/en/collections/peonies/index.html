Collection

Peonies — A Painting Collection

The sculptural petal, saturated colour and the impression of movement.

The peony unites the complex modelling of its petals, saturated colour and an impression of movement. The Peonies collection makes it possible to study the artistic interpretation of the flower through composition, texture and emotional expressiveness. Each work will be presented with the available information on its making, its material and the artist's intention.
