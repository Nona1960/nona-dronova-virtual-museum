Collection

The Energy of the Rose

The rose as concentrated energy: a single point at the centre and paint carried outward in a spiral. Acrylic on canvas.

The Energy of the Rose series is created layer by layer. Each new brushstroke enters into dialogue with the previous one, building a vibrant interplay of colour, light and contrast. The composition is not rigidly predetermined; it unfolds intuitively, like a musical improvisation.

At any moment the process can be paused and photographed: each stage has its own artistic integrity and can exist as an independent work. The collection therefore presents both finished works and states 'in progress'.
