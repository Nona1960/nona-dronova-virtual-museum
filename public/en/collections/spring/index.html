Collection

Spring — A Painting Collection

Renewal, changing light and the awakening of natural forms.

Spring is a theme of renewal, changing light and the awakening of natural forms. The collection invites the viewer to consider how impressions of spring find painterly expression in the works of Nona Dronova. Research commentaries will address colour relationships, compositional movement and images of seasonal transformation wherever the works themselves support them.

Not to reproduce what is before the eyes — but to make the viewer feel the air, light and joy of the moment.Nona Dronova
