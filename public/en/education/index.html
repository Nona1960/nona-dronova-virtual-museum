Artistic Thinking: Nona Dronova's Teaching Laboratory

Art education can begin with close observation of the world and continue in experiment with colour, form and material. The museum's teaching laboratory is devoted to the study of the creative process and to ways of developing independent visual thinking. It will gather verified materials from Nona Dronova's teaching, together with proposed curricula based on the study of nature, composition and the expressive possibilities of different materials.
