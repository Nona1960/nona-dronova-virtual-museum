About the artist

Nona Dronova — Artist and Researcher of Artistic Form

Nona Dronova's creative practice spans painting, artistic textiles, jewellery and the study of the garden as an aesthetic environment. The virtual museum presents these directions both in their independence and in their possible interconnections.

A special place belongs to the study of botanical imagery: colour, movement, the structure of plants and the ways in which they can be artistically transformed. The educational side of the project is concerned with passing on artistic experience and with developing observation and independent creative thinking.

Biographical information, exhibition history, education and publications will be presented as they are documented.
